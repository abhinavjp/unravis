# Unravis review pack - start here

Unravis is a read-only tool that helps find what makes our APIs slow. It reads the API audit log in MongoDB and shows evidence.

What is in this pack (read in this order):
1. README.md and MCP.md - what is built today and how to use it.
2. docs/api-audit-log-schema.md - the audit data shape.
3. docs/superpowers/specs/ - the MVP spec and the UI design direction.
4. docs/findings/ - the first review of the MVP.
5. docs/superpowers/plans/2026-10-23-*.md - the two plans already executed.
6. docs/superpowers/plans/2026-10-24-roadmap-review-plan.md - HOW to review the roadmap (three short sessions, decisions table, who reads what). Start here for what comes next.
7. docs/superpowers/plans/2026-10-24-unravis-post-mvp-roadmap.md - the proposed roadmap, **revision 9** (about 770 lines). Read the change log at the top and sections 1, 3.4 and 5 first, then one slice at a time.

Revisions 7 and 8 of the roadmap are superseded and are not in this pack.

Nothing in the roadmap is built, and it grants no access. Please review and challenge it before anything is implemented.
