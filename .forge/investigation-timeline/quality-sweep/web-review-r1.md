Scope: web/src files added or changed since 94ea98e for the investigation timeline (TracingChart, TraceToolbar, TracingPaper, lib/tracing/*, exportScrub, CaseHome/CaseTools wiring, tests). Every item below was verified by reading the code. "Covered?" refers to web/tests/*.test.cjs plus tools/e2e/specs/tracing-*.spec.ts.
