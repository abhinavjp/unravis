Prototype v3Slow call screen: a full page under the nav bar, not a pop-up▾ what changed

Why a full page, not a pop-up. This view holds a chart, two lists, a details panel and a data-source check, and you compare times across them for a long stretch. Pop-ups suit short single tasks. Long cross-referencing work needs room and its own address, so Back and Esc climb one level back to the timeline with the same window, like every other screen in the app.

What changed from v2. The Overview, Nearby calls and Nearby activity tabs are gone. The call's facts stay on screen, the timeline is always visible, Nearby calls and the all-sources list sit beside it on the same window, and one window control drives all of them. Click anything (a bar, a row) and the details panel shows it.

Use the pill at the bottom (or the ← → keys) to flip layouts. Numbers are invented and nothing is wired to the app. Esc closes this note.
