Create, open, archive, restore, free, erase

Every screen below is drawn with the real Unravis styles, fonts and wording style, with dummy data. The start screen, wizard and case home keep the layout the app has today. New parts (state chips, Case actions menu, Archive and Restore drawers, Free the space, Erase, case key) follow the same patterns. Screens are static; use the Theme and Width switches to check both themes and a phone.

Spec v7: buildout/specs/2026-10-26-case-lifecycle-spec.md. Message ids M1 to M19 are from its catalogue. Copy marked "sample" is new wording proposed for the build.
