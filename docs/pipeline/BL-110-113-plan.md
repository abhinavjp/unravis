## 8. Risks, biggest first
1. Parsing bytes from a target inside a SYSTEM service (step 3). Step 1 keeps the surface tiny and fixed (reply 28 bytes, preamble 102 bytes, rest counted and dropped). Step 2 is where the real exposure begins; its caps, fuzzing and the in-service versus helper-process decision are mandatory in that spec.
2. The condition-2 reading may forbid even the probe (option C). The owner is asked once; building D1-D6 first costs little and is reusable.
3. The real SYSTEM-to-IIS pipe ACL is only inferred. D8 and the owner run narrow it; a real IIS server closes it.
4. V2 on a host where PDH returns an unexpected status for a missing object: noise in `Missing`. The hosted-runner test is the guard.
5. Target-side cost is unmeasured until S4; nothing in this plan turns a session on in the service.
